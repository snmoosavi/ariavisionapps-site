Privacy Policy

Subtitle Generator Pro · Aria Vision
Effective date: July 24, 2026

Subtitle Generator Pro is a Windows desktop application published by Aria Vision. This policy explains how the application handles information.

Local processing

Subtitle Generator Pro is designed to process audio, video, subtitle, project, and configuration files locally on your Windows device. The application does not require an account and Aria Vision does not operate a server that receives your media, subtitle text, projects, or transcription results.

Information we collect

Aria Vision does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information through Subtitle Generator Pro.

The application stores settings, recent-file information, autosave data, recovery data, logs, and project files on your device so that its features can work. You control the media and subtitle files that you open, save, export, or share.

Network access and third-party services

When you choose a faster-whisper model that is not already available locally, the application may connect to Hugging Face infrastructure to download the selected model. Those requests are subject to the service provider's privacy practices. After download, transcription is performed locally.

The application uses FFmpeg and FFprobe locally for media inspection, audio extraction, subtitle rendering, and video export. These tools do not send your media to Aria Vision.

Microsoft Store and Windows may independently process purchase, diagnostic, crash, or usage information according to Microsoft's privacy terms. Aria Vision does not control that processing.

File access

Subtitle Generator Pro accesses only the files and folders that you select or that are required for application settings, logs, autosave, and crash recovery. It does not upload your media or subtitle content to Aria Vision.

Data retention and deletion

Local projects, exports, settings, model files, autosave files, recovery files, and logs remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the application. You can delete project and export files using Windows File Explorer. Application data can be removed by uninstalling the application and, when offered by Windows, choosing to remove its data.

Children

Subtitle Generator Pro is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children. Aria Vision knowingly collects no personal information from children through the application.

Security

We use a local-first design to reduce unnecessary transmission of user content. No method of storage is completely risk-free, so users should apply appropriate Windows account, device, backup, and file-security protections.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will be published at this URL.
