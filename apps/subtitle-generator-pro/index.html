Product story

Turn audio and video into polished subtitles locally.

Subtitle Generator Pro is designed for creators who need accurate captions without sending media to a cloud workflow.

Transcribe audio or video locally, review every segment in a subtitle editor, adjust timing, and style captions with a live video preview.

The app supports SRT, VTT, ASS, and TXT export, plus FFmpeg workflows for burning subtitles into video or creating supported soft-subtitle tracks.

Why users choose it

Subtitle creation with control over timing and style.

  • Keep media on the Windows device during transcription
  • Edit timing and text before export
  • Style captions with a live preview
  • Export subtitle files or burn captions into video

Workflow guide

A focused caption workflow from transcription to export.

Import a media file, choose the spoken language or automatic detection, then let the local transcription engine create editable subtitle segments.

Review the timeline, fix text, adjust timing, and use quality checks to catch overlaps, invalid durations, empty captions, or excessive reading speed.

Once captions are ready, export standard subtitle files or burn styled subtitles into a finished video for distribution.

Best-fit use cases

  • Local video transcription
  • Caption timing review
  • Styled subtitle export
  • Burned-in subtitles for video

FAQ

Questions about Subtitle Generator Pro

01

Does Subtitle Generator Pro work locally?

The Store description says transcription runs on your device; a network connection may only be needed to download a selected model that is not installed.

02

Which subtitle formats are supported?

The app description lists SRT, VTT, ASS, and TXT export, plus SRT, VTT, and ASS import.

03

Can it handle right-to-left subtitles?

Yes. The app includes automatic text direction support for RTL and LTR subtitles, including mixed text, numbers, and punctuation.

04

Where can I install Subtitle Generator Pro?
