Product story

Trade with a plan, review with evidence.

Trading Journal Pro helps traders move from scattered notes to a repeatable review process. Plan entries before execution, calculate position size, document context, and keep each setup connected to clear risk rules.

Use journal records, screenshots, setup tags, emotions, and R-multiple tracking to understand what is working and what needs attention. Reports, win rate, expectancy, drawdown, and equity curve views make performance easier to review over time.

The result is a focused Windows workspace for traders who want more discipline, better records, and clearer decisions after every session.
